OUTPUT FORMAT AND VERIFICATION
Mono 24,000 Hz Ogg Vorbis. Manifest records the exact transcript, measured
duration, file SHA256, bytes, voice settings and generator versions.
The generator checks tutorial-ID coverage, finite audio, decoded duration,
clipping/near-silence and hashes. These checks do not replace human listening.
Quest plays the committed audio without inference or a network service.
